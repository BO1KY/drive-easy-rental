Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need before you book.

What is the minimum driver age?

Drivers must be at least 21 years old, with a valid licence held for one year or more.

Which documents do I need?

A valid driver's licence, ID or passport and the payment card used for the booking.

Is a deposit required?

A refundable security hold is placed at pickup and released after the vehicle is returned.

What is the fuel policy?

Collect with a full tank and return full, or choose our convenient fuel option at checkout.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes — pending bookings can be cancelled free of charge from your account or via your confirmation code.
